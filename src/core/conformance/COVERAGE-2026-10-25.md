# Conformance evidence review, October 25, 2026

The corpus now has passing external reference layouts for **5 of the 55 registry
jurisdictions (9.1%)**. This is evidence for individual observed layouts.
Certification, physical print quality, every credential family and every issuer
edition require additional evidence and testing.

The 12 passing vectors cover nine jurisdictions in total. Four have only
synthetic baselines (CA, FL, TX, WA); 46 have no vector. The external references
cover AR v09 DL, CT v09 DL, GA v09 DL, IL v08 DL and NY v10 DL. Versions 08, 09

Islands and Canadian jurisdictions are outside this denominator.

## Three source-byte matches

- **Illinois v08:** the Secretary of State's
  [Central Issuance brochure](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/dsd_a301.pdf),
  PDF page 2, provides a 354-byte sample with DL and ZI subfiles. The barcode
  contains older and different details from the adjacent updated card front.
  The fixture uses the barcode itself.
- **Georgia v09:** DDS's
  [June 2019 barcode guide](https://dds.georgia.gov/document/publication/license-printed-beginning-june-2019/download)
  supplies a 400-byte sample with jurisdiction version 01 and ZG data. Its final
  elements end directly with CR. The height uses lowercase `in`; opaque ZG
  fields retain their source case. The sample's ten-year date interval triggers
  the existing strict-mode validity advisory, so this fixture checks normal
  generation with validation enabled.
- **Arkansas v09:** a public State Bank Department copy of DFA's 2018 calibration
  document supplies a 310-byte sample with ZA/ZAA. Its live URL returns 404.
  The [archived government PDF](https://web.archive.org/web/20200919023843id_/http://banking.arkansas.gov/public/userfiles/Important_Info/Arkansas_Barcode_Specifications_2018.pdf)
  was captured on September 19, 2020. The fixture preserves the recovered bytes.

Each new profile is scoped to the observed AAMVA version. The fixture values
remain input to the encoder; the encoder calculates directories and serializes
changed data. It does not replay a stored reference payload.

## Source fidelity changes the claim

The Connecticut fixture's own provenance describes a decoded field dump,
uncertain trailing padding, a repaired directory boundary and an omitted empty
ZCA element. Its passing result proves a canonicalized derived layout. The two
New York fixtures preserve observed structure while replacing identifying
values. These remain useful evidence with explicit fidelity labels.

`conformance:report` inventories references and validates provenance using the
same loader as the tests. It does not execute the encoder. `conformance.test.ts`
checks byte comparisons and `scanOracle.test.ts` independently reads rendered
symbols with ZXing. Those checks answer different questions.

## New edition review

The [October 2026 AAMVA standard](https://www.aamva.org/getmedia/feab821e-baa5-4524-b865-3eb172b51acd/2026-DLID-Card-Design-Standard.pdf)
supersedes 2025 and assigns version 12 in Table D.1. Annex D adds SIG and SCV.
Annex B specifies ECDSA/SHA-256, a P-256 key, fixed 64-byte r||s, and an
80-character ASCII85 representation without z substitution. Partial coverage
must retain the original header and directory, omit whole unsigned elements
from the canonical string, and always sign SCV itself.

The D.13 example has the discrepancies listed above. Inspection of the PDF and
an independent ASCII85 decode establish the length and DER-format mismatch.
A limited check of 48 plausible transcription variants did not establish a
valid signature under the supplied public key, using either raw or DER
interpretation. That search does not prove every conceivable reconstruction
fails. The normative requirements and example must stay separate in test data.

The 2005 and 2016 copies reviewed here identify AAMVA as author but are hosted
on third-party mirrors. Their original authenticity was not independently
attested. The archived Arkansas PDF is an identified government publication
preserved by an archive, rather than a newly published 2026 calibration sample.

## What completeness still requires

For each intended issuer, edition and credential family, obtain an authorized,
readable marked sample or consented anonymized source, preserve its bytes and
provenance, and demonstrate generator parity against the independent source.
Add source-fidelity labels and raise the coverage ratchet only after the
corresponding tests pass. Front-only artwork supplies visual evidence.

The accompanying local report inventories all 55 jurisdictions, all observed
payloads, acquisition attempts and remaining gaps. American Samoa and Puerto
Rico remain open for image/reference acquisition. Forty-three jurisdictions
lack a public payload in this intake; fifty lack a passing external reference.
No evidence in this review establishes full conformance.
